I'm a second-year PhD student advised by Dr. Joel Zylberberg at UCLA. I study how artificial neural networks can learn good representations without end-to-end backpropagation, and I want to better understand when local, brain-inspired learning rules work.

My most recent work showed that widening a network lets greedy layer-wise self-supervised learning match or even beat end-to-end backprop. I'm currently building fully local, spiking networks that perform self-supervised learning on real sensory inputs.

Before this, I worked on hippocampal place cells in the Wikenheiser Lab (UCLA), studied dopamine circuits in the Hollon Lab (UCSD), and built automated EEG analysis pipelines in the Mucke Lab (Gladstone/UCSF).

I'm looking for a research internship for summer 2027.
